## Overview
The charger, or charging system, is the electromechanical assembly responsible for charging the accumulator (battery pack). It accepts AC power from an EV supply equipment using the J1772 interface and converts it to DC through the Elcon charger before delivering power to the accumulator. Broadly speaking, the charging system functions as a simplified counterpart to the vehicle powertrain during charging of the accumulator.
  
The system incorporates many of the same subsystems found on the car’s powertrain, including high-voltage distribution, low-voltage control, shutdown circuitry, and safety interlocks. Because high voltage is present throughout the charging system, it contains several safety-critical elements. The shutdown circuit in particular must be present and fully functional to ensure safe operation.

The primary goals for ARG26 include efficient mechanical packaging and harness routing, a reliable and robust electrical system, secure mounting to the charging cart during competition use, and comprehensive documentation detailing the design and operation of the charging system.
